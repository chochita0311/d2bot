# Reviewed sorceress appearance

[manifest.json](manifest.json) owns the reusable cyan player marker, four reviewed body crops, Energy Shield association, search/crop geometry, thresholds, tracking limits and 21 positive/negative surrounding reference crops. Images moved from the old personal buff owner without changing their bytes or calibration values. Key bindings, character/account names and buff order remain in personal configuration/history.

These samples cover one observed sorceress appearance in the native 1922×1140 remastered Korean window. The current body and same-frame Energy Shield support an optional marker-free fallback when they match uniquely. Body-template similarity, blurred appearance and distinct vertical foreground remain different evidence types; foreground-only results have no template confidence. The observer does not substitute a past point or fixed screen center, and ambiguous candidates remain unknown.

The cyan marker and Energy Shield are appearance candidates. They do not prove physical ground projection, every pose/stair/combat condition, all buff effects, terrain safety, enemy clearance, equipment restoration or independent-room accuracy. Replay matches alone grant no live approval. The reviewed [camera layout](../../../ui/layouts/remastered-1922x1140/README.md) is a separate UI calibration with current viewport/HUD requirements.

`templates/body/` holds the four body crops. The 21 surrounding crops in `references/` retain the selected marker/ES states; the manifest's `ground_references` preserves source hashes, canvas boxes, visual review and original reader results. Visual review and replay are not independent validations. Nine full sequence raws were removed after this selected calibration coverage was retained. The existing manifest's `source_retention` and personal sequence report preserve each historical filename/SHA and explicit `source_image_retained=false`; timing, HUD and point results remain historical observations, rather than claims that raw pixels are still available.
