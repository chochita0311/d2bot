# Shared Survival HUD Assets

[manifest.json](manifest.json) owns the reviewed `remastered-ko-window-1922x1140` layout and matching limits used by the [observer](../../../diablo2/common/survival_vision.py). These shared assets contain HUD labels, digit/slash masks, belt edges and item appearances. Character names, bindings, FCR, thresholds and belt policy belong to personal configuration. Generic town/menu assets now live in the [shared town owner](../town-loop/remastered-ko-window/README.md); unnecessary name-bearing source images were removed after reusable UI crops were preserved.

## Provenance

`closed_belt_slots` reads only the four currently visible bottom-row slots after positive closure evidence. It can identify a configured potion column for the common [emergency recovery consumer](../../../diablo2/common/emergency_recovery.py); it never supplies hidden-row counts. That consumer requests one potion key, verifies changed current/max ratios, then reads the expanded belt and closes it. Key dispatch alone does not establish consumption or remaining stock. Main integration is implemented; its actual recovery use remains unverified in the current integration run.

The `2026-10-05` Act 1 town capture supplied labels, the 985/985 and 1075/1075 digits, belt row appearances and one empty upper slot. Existing [public HUD reference](references/number-four-source.png) supplied 3/4 glyphs. The same session's character panel supplied 2/6 masks. Revision v5 adds actual HUD 4/6 variants; v9 adds actual HUD 2. Glyph extraction retains only the numeral masks.

Revision `screen-reference-2026-10-05-v2` adds five HUD crops from one supervised Arcane central-waypoint buff sequence: before, weapon swap, before Orders, after Orders, and restored battle equipment. Life maxima were 985, 895, 895, 1490, and 1580; mana maxima were 1075 before Orders and 1543 afterward. These samples support the observed numbers and label shifts, not autonomous skill or field-safety recognition.

`references/` contains those five field crops plus the four town crops: expanded, eleven potions, closed, and restored. Each image is 1400×247 and is embedded at `canvas_box` in a blank 1922×1140 canvas by replay tests. Counts and resource pairs are the human-reviewed expected values in the manifest. Samples from the same scene are follow-up observations, not independent rooms. Closed field crops have unknown hidden-row counts.

The observer searches for a reviewed label within its bounded region before reading adjacent digits. The buffed mana-label variant handles the captured rendering difference without lowering the label threshold. `belt-closed-edge.png` supplies positive closed-state evidence; failure to match an expanded belt alone does not prove closure.

Full new screenshots, OCR probes and intermediate crops were task-owned diagnostics and were removed after extracting these durable references. No runtime or test depends on those temporary files.

## Calibration Limits

Revision `screen-reference-2026-10-09-v6` adds the reviewed main north-travel HUD crop `field-north-life-slash.png` and its slash raster variant. The previous atlas rejected the 19×10 separator while every digit remained readable. The .72 score and .08 margin remain unchanged, and the obscured town separator remains rejected. The stored life/mana pairs are same-frame replay expectations, never fixed resource thresholds.

Revision `screen-reference-2026-10-09-v7` adds `references/field-north-background.png`, the reviewed fourth-landing HUD from main development attempt46. Bright terrain overlapped the life label and separator. Label recovery compares the glyph and its immediate outline at the original candidate location, retaining the .90 label limit. Separator recovery applies only to one ambiguous internal glyph surrounded by positively read digits and gaps of at least6px on both sides; it tests at most2 rows/1 column of edge noise and retains the .72 score/.08 margin. It never repairs digits. The obscured town separator and a removed field separator remain rejected. A missing label no longer discards an independently readable resource, but travel still requires both same-frame ratios. Replaying14 main40/46 frames reads both resources; independent rooms and the revised live path remain unverified. Orb-fill ratio observation is not implemented.

- Label/glyph scores are mask similarities; item/edge distances are normalized color differences. They are calibration limits, not probabilities or measured safety guarantees.
- Purple samples show the large full-rejuvenation item. Small rejuvenation potions and other graphics are unsupported. Upper/middle/base references preserve actual row appearance differences.
- Only one real upper empty slot was captured. Other empty positions, combat overlays, poison, resource values beyond the collected samples, other layouts/languages and independent field accuracy require further data.
- Keep coordinates, template shapes and revision together when adding a reviewed layout. Do not resize unknown layouts to fit or lower limits to force ambiguous reads. Unknown slots/numbers remain unknown.

See [usage and evidence limits](../../../docs/features/survival-observation.md), the [town record](../../../docs/plans/run/run-20261005-03-survival-screen-observation.md), and the [field record](../../../docs/plans/run/run-20261005-04-field-buff-confirmation.md).

Revision v8's opt-in high-orb fallback provides only a conservative .90 lower bound when every reviewed interior row is at least85% liquid-colored. It does not fabricate current/maximum numbers or handle arbitrary fill levels. Revision `screen-reference-2026-10-10-v10` adds the common HUD crop `field-mana-leading-edge-speck.png` from main176: life1578/1580 and mana1289/1543 are replay expectations.
